## 1. FRONTEND DESIGN SKILL — INDUSTRIAL SOLAR & WHOLESALE ARCHITECTURE
Apply these non-negotiable visual rules across the entire Homepage and PublicShell:

1. **BANNED "AI SLOP" PATTERNS (Remove Immediately):**
   - NO glowing background blobs (`blur-2xl`, `blur-3xl`, radial glow divs).
   - NO bubbly `rounded-3xl` or `rounded-2xl` cards with heavy drop shadows (`shadow-2xl`). Use crisp architectural `rounded-md` or `rounded-lg` with sharp `1px` borders (`border-slate-200` on light, `border-slate-800` on dark).
   - NO rainbow icon badges or centered cartoon-style cards. Left-align all card content with technical precision.
   - NO gradient text (`bg-clip-text text-transparent bg-gradient-to-r`). Use solid, high-contrast typography (`text-slate-950` on light, `text-white` on dark).
   - NO generic marketing fluff ("Revolutionizing the future of green energy"). Use concrete Pakistan solar B2B terminology (`N-Type TOPCon`, `IP65 Dual-MPPT`, `LiFePO4 6000+ Cycles`, `PESCO Net-Metering`, `Container & Pallet Wholesale`).

2. **TYPOGRAPHY & SWISS GRID DISCIPLINE:**
   - **Headings:** Tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), bold weight (`font-bold`), crisp line-height (`leading-[1.12]`).
   - **Technical Specs, Numbers & Index Labels:** Always use `font-mono` (`tabular-nums`) for wattages (`580W–710W`), capacities (`6kW`), stats (`1,250+`), and section index numbers (`01 // CORE HARDWARE`).
   - **Exposed Hairline Grids:** Use structural `1px` borders (`border-y border-slate-200`, `divide-x divide-slate-200`) so sections look like an engineered datasheet/catalogue rather than floating boxes.

3. **COLOR PALETTE (High-Contrast Daylight + Deep Slate):**
   - **Primary Canvas:** Crisp Daylight White (`#FFFFFF`) and Technical Alabaster (`#F8FAFC` / `bg-slate-50`) for main content sections so hardware photos pop cleanly.
   - **Authority Dark Sections:** Deep Carbon Slate (`#090D16` / `bg-slate-950`) strictly for the Top Utility Bar, Hero Overlay, Stats Bar, Projects Reel, and Footer.
   - **Primary Accent:** Industrial Solar Amber (`#D97706` / `bg-amber-600` on light, `#F59E0B` / `bg-amber-500` on dark).
